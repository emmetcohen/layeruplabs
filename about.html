About

Hi, I’m Emmet.

Lutz, Florida

Next to my digital artwork at a gallery.

I have a passion for design and building things that work. I taught myself Blender and 3D printing, and I use them for both engineering and art.

Layer Up Labs is the studio I started in 2023. I model, prototype and fabricate functional parts: an RC car and a 6 ft RC battleship that I redesigned in Blender every time a part failed in testing. I’m headed for engineering because I like making things and finding out why they fail.
