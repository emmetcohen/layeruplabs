About

Hi, I’m Emmet.

Lutz, Florida

Next to my digital artwork at a gallery.

I got my first 3D printer when I was twelve, and I figured out quickly that the interesting problems live in the gap between a model and a real object.

Layer Up Labs is the studio I started in 2023. I design in Blender and ZBrush, print on an FDM printer, and finish everything by hand. I’m headed for mechanical engineering because I like making things and finding out why they fail.
