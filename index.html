How A Thing Gets Made

Screen to shelf

Model

The shape is built and sculpted in Blender. Nothing here is a downloaded file.

Slice

Orientation, wall count, and supports get decided. The choices that determine whether it survives.

Print

Hours on the plate, laid down a fraction of a millimeter at a time.

Finish

Supports removed, seams sanded, hardware fitted. This is the part that separates a print from a product.
