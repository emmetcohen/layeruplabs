How a part gets made

Model

Blender and ZBrush. Every part starts as geometry on a screen.

Slice

Turn the model into layers and decide how it will actually be built.

Print

FDM and resin. This is where the model finds out what it got wrong.

Finish

Support removal, sanding, assembly, paint.
