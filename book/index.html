FAQ

Good to know

When do I get my photos?

Usually within a week. You’ll get a link to a private online gallery where you can view and download everything.

Where do we shoot?

Outdoor spots around the Tampa Bay area, picked for good light. I’ll send a few options once your date is booked, or we can shoot somewhere that matters to you.

What should we wear?

Solid colors and simple outfits photograph best. Skip big logos and busy patterns. For families, coordinate colors instead of matching exactly.

What if it rains?

We reschedule for free. Florida weather happens.

How do I pay?

I’ll send payment details when we confirm your date. Payment is due at the session.

Do you travel?

Anywhere in the Tampa Bay area. Farther than that may add a small travel fee, which I’ll tell you up front.
